## Selection and application

Choose a template from the home page, then use any creation method. The validated template ID is carried through schedule entry and review. On the explicit transition to Studio, the recipe applies once and the template query is consumed.

Recipes contain design and target configuration only. They never contain or replace schedule data, student text, project metadata, or uploaded assets. Applying a recipe creates or reuses the Android Phone 1080 × 2400 target, clears target-only overrides, keeps other device targets intact, and makes one normal undo/autosave commit.

Every beta recipe uses automatic subject colors, no bundled stickers, an intentional typography pairing, and one of the available renderer layouts. The home-page previews are lightweight HTML representations derived from the same theme and typography data as the applied wallpaper rather than separate static screenshots. Preview subject codes come from multiple curricula already bundled with ScheduleBud, and the cards vary their timetable arrangement instead of sharing one copied placeholder.

## Design basis

The collection uses a distinctive display face for titles and a quieter sans serif for schedule details. Palettes favor one dominant family, one controlled accent, and readable foreground/background contrast. Gradient recipes use perceptual multi-stop blending, aspect-ratio-aware radial fields, and softened three-color conic centers. School-inspired directions are based on public institutional color references for AdZU blue and white, WMSU crimson and white, Universidad de Zamboanga moss green and white, and ZPPSU maroon and gold.
